Privacy Policy for Peyo

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy explains what Peyo ("the app") does with your data. Peyo is an expense tracker built to work fully offline, with an optional account for backup.

App package: com.peyo.app

1. Summary

Everything you record in Peyo is written to a private database on your own phone first. An account is optional and exists only to back that data up and bring it to a second phone; the app is fully usable, offline, without one. Peyo does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking SDKs, does not sell or share your data with advertisers, and does not read your SMS messages, notifications, call log, or Google Pay account.

2. Information Peyo stores on your device

The following is stored only in a local, app-private database on your phone, and is not visible to other apps:

  • Expenses you record: amount, category, merchant/payee, your note, date and time.
  • People you add to split expenses with, and the split amounts, percentages, or equal shares you assign them.
  • Categories you create or rename.
  • Settlement status of each shared expense (settled or outstanding).
  • Receipt screenshots and other images you attach to an expense. These are copied into private app storage on your device and are never uploaded, including when the app has an account signed in.
  • Your chosen display currency and app theme.

Receipts and attachments are excluded from Android's automatic device backup and are excluded from every sync and local export described below. They never leave your device by any feature this app provides.

3. On-device receipt and statement reading (OCR)

When you share a payment screenshot with Peyo, or import a bank or UPI statement PDF, the app uses Google's ML Kit on-device text recognition to read the amount, payee, and note out of the image. This recognition runs entirely on your phone; the image and the text extracted from it are not sent to Google, to us, or to any server. You review and can edit every field before anything is saved.

4. Contacts

Peyo requests the READ_CONTACTS permission only when you choose to import people from your phone's contact list, from the People section of Settings. If granted:

  • Only contact names are read, to offer them as people to add.
  • Nothing is written back to your contacts, and no contact data (names, phone numbers, or anything else) is sent off your device.
  • You choose which names, if any, to add before anything is saved.

You can decline this permission and add people by typing their names instead; every other feature of the app works without it.

5. Optional account and cloud backup

Creating an account is entirely optional and is offered so your data can survive a reinstall or reach a second phone. If Peyo is built with a backend configured, and you choose to sign in or create an account, the following is sent to that backend (Supabase, described in section 6):

  • Your email address, used to authenticate you.
  • Your password, handled by the authentication provider; the app itself never stores or transmits it in plain form beyond the sign-in request.
  • Your expenses, categories, people, and split records, as described in section 2, excluding receipt images and other attachments, which never leave your device.

This sync happens automatically a few seconds after you record or edit something, and again when the app opens, unless you turn automatic backup off in Settings; a manual "Back up now" button is available either way. Restoring reads that same data back down onto a device, for example after reinstalling the app.

If Peyo is built without a backend configured, the account screens are hidden entirely and no network request of this kind is ever made; the app runs wholly offline and only the local file backup described in section 7 is available.

You can sign out and stop syncing at any time from Settings. You can also delete your account entirely, in the app or by email — see our Account Deletion page for exact steps. Deleting your account removes it and everything it backed up from the server; it does not touch what is on your phone.

6. Third-party service: Supabase

Account creation, sign-in, and cloud backup, where offered, are handled by Supabase, a backend service. Supabase stores your account credentials and the transaction data described in section 5 on its infrastructure. Row-level security rules restrict each account to its own data. Supabase's own privacy practices govern data once it reaches their servers; see supabase.com/privacy for details. Peyo sends this service only the data described in section 5, nothing more, and only when you have chosen to use an account.

No other third-party service, analytics platform, advertising network, or crash-reporting tool receives any data from this app.

7. Local file backup

Independent of any account, you can write a backup file containing your expenses, people, and categories to a location you choose on your device (or anywhere your device's file picker can reach, such as removable storage or a cloud-storage folder you have already set up on the phone), and import such a file back in. This feature works fully offline, requires no account, and Peyo does not transmit this file anywhere on your behalf; where it ends up is entirely up to the location you pick. Receipts and attachments are not included in this file.

8. Permissions Peyo requests

  • READ_CONTACTS: to offer names from your phone's contact list when you choose to import people. Requested only when you use that feature; see section 4.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: used only for the optional account sign-in, backup, and restore described in section 5. Peyo works fully offline without them being exercised.

Peyo does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, SMS, call log, notifications, or storage beyond the receipt images you explicitly attach and the backup file you explicitly choose to write or read.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it.
  • Deleting an expense, person, or category in the app removes it from your device and, if you have an account, marks it deleted so the deletion also reaches the account's backup on your next sync.
  • Uninstalling Peyo removes its local database and every attachment stored on your device. If you have an account, that account and its backed-up data remain in the backend until you delete the account — see Account Deletion.

10. Children's privacy

Peyo is a general-purpose expense tracker and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Security

Data on your device is stored in this app's private storage, which other apps cannot read without your device being compromised. Data sent to the optional backend travels over an encrypted connection and is restricted to your account by row-level security rules. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and this policy does not guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
