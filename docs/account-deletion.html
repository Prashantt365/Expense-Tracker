Delete your Peyo account and data

Peyo is an offline-first expense tracker. Account deletion below covers the optional cloud account only — nothing on your phone requires it.

Option 1: Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open Peyo and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Under Account, find the row showing your signed-in email.
  3. Tap Delete account, below the sign-out icon.
  4. Read the confirmation and tap Delete account again to confirm.

This removes your account and everything it backed up from our server immediately — no waiting period, no separate request. You will be signed out. Nothing on your phone is touched; your local expenses, people, categories and receipts stay exactly where they were, since that data never required an account to begin with.

Option 2: Ask us to delete it for you

If you can no longer access the app, or would rather someone else handle it, email us from the address your account is registered under and we will delete it on your behalf.

Email YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL@example.com

We aim to complete manually requested deletions within 30 days.

What gets deleted, and what doesn't

Deleted from our serverYour account (email, password), and every expense, category, person and split you backed up to it.
Not affectedAnything stored on your phone: your expenses, categories, people, and receipt photos or screenshots. Peyo works fully offline, so this data is yours regardless of whether you ever had an account, and deleting the account does not erase or change it. Receipts are never uploaded in the first place, so there is nothing of theirs to delete on our end.
When it happensImmediately for the in-app option; within 30 days for an emailed request.
