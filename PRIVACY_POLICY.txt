PRIVACY POLICY FOR PEYO

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy explains what Peyo ("the app") does with your data. Peyo is an
expense tracker built to work fully offline, with an optional account for
backup. This document describes the app's behavior as built; if you are
publishing Peyo under your own name or organization, replace the bracketed
placeholders below with your own details before distributing it.

1. SUMMARY

Everything you record in Peyo is written to a private database on your own
phone first. An account is optional and exists only to back that data up and
bring it to a second phone; the app is fully usable, offline, without one.
Peyo does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking SDKs, does not
sell or share your data with advertisers, and does not read your SMS
messages, notifications, call log, or Google Pay account.


2. INFORMATION PEYO STORES ON YOUR DEVICE

The following is stored only in a local, app-private database on your phone,
and is not visible to other apps:

  - Expenses you record: amount, category, merchant/payee, your note, date
    and time.
  - People you add to split expenses with, and the split amounts,
    percentages, or equal shares you assign them.
  - Categories you create or rename.
  - Settlement status of each shared expense (settled or outstanding).
  - Receipt screenshots and other images you attach to an expense. These are
    copied into private app storage on your device and are never uploaded,
    including when the app has an account signed in.
  - Your chosen display currency and app theme.

Receipts and attachments are excluded from Android's automatic device backup
and are excluded from every sync and local export described below. They
never leave your device by any feature this app provides.


3. ON-DEVICE RECEIPT AND STATEMENT READING (OCR)

When you share a payment screenshot with Peyo, or import a bank or UPI
statement PDF, the app uses Google's ML Kit on-device text recognition to
read the amount, payee, and note out of the image. This recognition runs
entirely on your phone; the image and the text extracted from it are not
sent to Google, to us, or to any server. You review and can edit every
field before anything is saved.


4. CONTACTS

Peyo requests the READ_CONTACTS permission only when you choose to import
people from your phone's contact list, from the People section of Settings.
If granted:

  - Only contact names are read, to offer them as people to add. Phone
    numbers, email addresses and every other contact detail are never read.
  - Nothing is written back to your contacts.
  - You choose which names, if any, to add before anything is saved. Names
    you do not add are discarded and never leave your device.
  - A name you add becomes a person in Peyo like one you typed. If you use
    the optional account described in section 5, your people are backed up
    with the rest of your data, so an added name is sent to that backend
    along with them.

You can decline this permission and add people by typing their names
instead; every other feature of the app works without it.


5. OPTIONAL ACCOUNT AND CLOUD BACKUP

Creating an account is entirely optional and is offered so your data can
survive a reinstall or reach a second phone. If Peyo is built with a backend
configured, and you choose to sign in or create an account, the following is
sent to that backend (Supabase, described in section 6):

  - Your email address, used to authenticate you.
  - Your password, handled by the authentication provider; the app itself
    never stores or transmits it in plain form beyond the sign-in request.
  - Your expenses, categories, people, and split records, as described in
    section 2, EXCLUDING receipt images and other attachments, which never
    leave your device.

This sync happens automatically a few seconds after you record or edit
something, and again when the app opens, unless you turn automatic backup
off in Settings; a manual "Back up now" button is available either way.
Restoring reads that same data back down onto a device, for example after
reinstalling the app.

If Peyo is built without a backend configured, the account options are
shown as unavailable and no network request of this kind is ever made; the app
runs wholly offline and only the local file backup described in section 7 is
available.

You can sign out and stop syncing at any time from Settings. You can also
delete your account entirely from Settings, under Account: this permanently
removes your account and every row it owns from the backend -- your
expenses, categories, people, and splits as backed up there -- right away,
with no separate request needed. It does not touch what is on your device;
you are simply signed out, and the data already on your phone remains
yours to keep or export locally. If you would rather ask us to do this for
you, or run into trouble with the in-app option, contact
[YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL ADDRESS].


6. THIRD-PARTY SERVICE: SUPABASE

Account creation, sign-in, and cloud backup, where offered, are handled by
Supabase (supabase.com), a backend service. Supabase stores your account
credentials and the transaction data described in section 5 on its
infrastructure. Row-level security rules restrict each account to its own
data. Supabase's own privacy practices govern data once it reaches their
servers; see supabase.com/privacy for details. Peyo sends this service only
the data described in section 5, nothing more, and only when you have
chosen to use an account.

No other third-party service, analytics platform, advertising network, or
crash-reporting tool receives any data from this app.


7. LOCAL FILE BACKUP

Independent of any account, you can write a backup file containing your
expenses, people, and categories to a location you choose on your device (or
anywhere your device's file picker can reach, such as removable storage or a
cloud-storage folder you have already set up on the phone), and import such
a file back in. This feature works fully offline, requires no account, and
Peyo does not transmit this file anywhere on your behalf; where it ends up
is entirely up to the location you pick. Receipts and attachments are not
included in this file.


8. PERMISSIONS PEYO REQUESTS

  - READ_CONTACTS: to offer names from your phone's contact list when you
    choose to import people. Requested only when you use that feature; see
    section 4.
  - INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: used only for the optional account
    sign-in, backup, and restore described in section 5. Peyo works fully
    offline without them being exercised.

Peyo does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, SMS,
call log, notifications, or storage beyond the receipt images you
explicitly attach and the backup file you explicitly choose to write or
read.


9. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

  - Data on your device stays until you delete it in the app, clear the
    app's storage, or uninstall it.
  - Deleting an expense, person, or category in the app removes it from
    your device and, if you have an account, marks it deleted so the
    deletion also reaches the account's backup on your next sync.
  - Uninstalling Peyo removes its local database and every attachment
    stored on your device. If you have an account, that account and its
    backed-up data remain in the backend until you delete the account, in
    Settings or by contacting us, as described in section 5.


10. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Peyo is a general-purpose expense tracker and is not directed at children.
It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.


11. SECURITY

Data on your device is stored in this app's private storage, which other
apps cannot read without your device being compromised. Data sent to the
optional backend travels over an encrypted connection and is restricted to
your account by row-level security rules. No method of storage or
transmission is perfectly secure, and this policy does not guarantee
absolute security.
